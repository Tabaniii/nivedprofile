# 1. Overview & Objective

The **NFC Public Web** is a very lightweight middleware/landing page application accessed by a customer when they tap their phone on an NFC card, or scan a QR code, placed at a table or cashier counter of a small/medium business (UMKM).

## Primary Goals

- **Automatic interaction telemetry:** Record physical tap/scan interaction data automatically — timestamp, OS type, and device type.
- **Reputation Shield:** Protect the store's digital reputation through a star-rating (1–5) filtering system before any review reaches a public platform.
- **Speed:** Keep page load time under **1 second** so there is no delay that causes the customer to abandon the review process.
